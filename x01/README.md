# X01 - how to play

X01 is the classic countdown darts game. Start from **301** or **501** and race to exactly **0**.

## Setup

1. Add players **in throwing order**.
2. Pick the starting score: **301** or **501**.
3. Pick the start condition: **Direct in** or **Double in**.
4. Pick the end condition: **Direct out** or **Double out**.
5. Pick **Single game** or a best-of match.
6. Press **Start game**.

## Gameplay

- Each turn is one **three-dart round total** from 0 to 180.
- Press **Confirm turn** to subtract the entered score from the player's remaining score.
- Press **Busted** when the turn should not count.
- In **Double in**, only confirm a score once the player has opened on a double.
- In **Double out**, players must finish on a double. Bullseye **50** counts as double 25.

## Busts

The app disables **Confirm turn** for numeric busts:

- the entered score is higher than the player's remaining score;
- double-out scoring would leave the player on 1.

Players should still press **Busted** for rule busts that cannot be checked from a round total, such as a failed double-in attempt or a non-double finishing dart in double-out.

## End of game

- A game ends when a player reaches exactly **0**.
- In a best-of match, the first player to the required number of game wins takes the match.
