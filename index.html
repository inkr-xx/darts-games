Follow the wedge

Seven rounds of 501-style tracking: the round target (a wedge or bull) is set by the first scoring hit, then everyone plays on that target. Turn order for round 1 is random; later rounds go lowest score first.

Random wedge

Rounds 1–6 use a random wedge (1–20) each time; round 7 is always bull. Wedge rounds score 1 / 2 / 3 per dart; bull rounds score 5 or 15. Standings use triple / double / single counts when totals tie.

Around the world

Single player: hit all 20 wedges in order (1→20, 20→1, or a shuffled random order). Your score is the number of darts taken; each round offers up to three darts.
