# Around the world — how to play

Single-player practice game: hit every wedge **in order**. Your **score is the total number of darts thrown** (lower is better).

## Setup

1. Enter your **name** (saved with other games for “last names”).
2. Choose **1 → 20**, **20 → 1**, or **Random** (all 20 wedges in an order shuffled once at start). In **Random**, a **progress bar** shows how many wedges you’ve cleared (**n / 20**).
3. Press **Start game**.

## Gameplay

- Each **round** allows up to **three darts**.
- Tap **Hit** / **Miss** for each dart (same row). The **pending** line appears between the Hit/Miss row and the Confirm/Reset row (each entry includes the wedge, e.g. **Hit 4 → Miss 5**) — **Darts thrown** in the header updates immediately to include pending darts; **Confirm** locks them in, **Reset** clears pending and the score preview.
- **Confirm** applies your pending throws for this stretch in order (updates score and board).
- **Reset** clears pending only — start again if you tapped the wrong outcome before confirming.
- The board shows a **compact dartboard**: this round’s wedges are **highlighted** on the chart, with **numbers beside it** in order (for example **7 → 8 → 9**).
- You must hit the **current** wedge before the next. Any **single, double, or triple** on that wedge counts as a **hit**.
- **Bull** does not count — record **Miss** (the dart still counts toward your total).
- A dart that lands on a **different wedge** than the one you need is a **miss** (still counts).
- After **three confirmed** darts in a round, the next round starts.
- **N/N** appears only when this round shows **one or two** wedge targets (not three): then you can end the round early so **unused darts are not thrown** and do **not** add to your score (after at least one dart in that round). When all **three** wedge slots are shown for the round, **N/N** is hidden — use all three darts or advance by hitting/missing until the round ends automatically.

## End of game

When the **final wedge in your mode’s sequence** is hit (including the last step of a **Random** order), the game ends. If you finish on the **first or second** dart of a round, any **remaining darts for that round are not used** and do **not** count.
